## Compliance

- Figure width 7.00 in (504 pt): at the ACS double-column limit; height well under the 9.167 in maximum.
- Raster resolution 600 dpi, above the 300 dpi minimum for colour artwork.
- Font Arial throughout; smallest text 4.8 pt at final size, above the 4.5 pt floor.
- Panel letters (a)-(d) are part of the artwork, not the caption.
- TOC_Graphic is 3.25 x 1.75 in, the ACS TOC/abstract graphic size.
